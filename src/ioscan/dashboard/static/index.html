      Marker plate, calibration and scanning

      1 · Prepare the two sheets (once)

      1. Open the marker sheet (Setup → Calibration → Open PDF) and print two copies at 100 % ("Actual size", not "Fit to page") on matt paper.
      2. Check the 100 mm bar with a ruler. Note the measured length.
      3. Cut along the dashed line and glue each copy flat on a stiff card. No tape or glossy film over the markers.
      4. Copy A stays bare: it is the calibration sheet. Copy B is the model plate: fix the model on its middle with tack or double-sided tape so it cannot shift.

      2 · Calibrate (once, and again if the camera, lens or laser is moved)

      1. Check the camera. Put the bare calibration sheet under the scanner where the model will be.
      2. In Setup → Calibration enter the measured bar length and press Record calibration.
      3. For the 30 seconds, move the sheet slowly: slide it around, lift it 1–2 cm and lower it, and tilt it by lifting each edge in turn. The laser line must stay on the sheet.
      4. Wait for "Calibration saved". A sheet residual below 0.15 mm is good. If it says the sheet was not tilted enough, repeat with larger tilts.

      3 · Scan

      1. Put the model plate under the scanner. In the Scan tab the Ready check should show 4 or more markers.
      2. Press Start scan. Slide and turn the plate so the laser line crosses every tooth, from the last molar on one side around to the last molar on the other.
      3. You may move at any speed, stop, go back, tilt or lift the plate: the markers measure it. Just keep at least two markers visible and your fingers off the print.
      4. Press Stop. The result is marked Good, Usable or Rescan, with the reason.

      4 · Tooth sides and combining scans

      1. For the side walls, rest the plate on a wedge (about 30°) so the cheek side faces the laser and scan again; repeat for the tongue side.
      2. In the Library, tick the scans made on the same plate and press Build arch. They are combined directly, with no stitching, as long as the model did not move on the plate.

      Lighting

      Shade the scanner from direct room light and keep one small, steady lamp nearby. Background light should read below about 90 in the Ready check. A matte spray on glossy plastic teeth gives a much stronger laser line.
